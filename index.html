    How to play

    Drag blocks onto the 8x8 stone slab.
    Fill a full row or column to clear it. Gem stones in a cleared line are collected.
    Journey: free every gem before your moves run out.
    Zen: endless, chase your best. Daily: the same board for everyone.
    Clear several lines at once, and on consecutive moves, for combo bonuses.
