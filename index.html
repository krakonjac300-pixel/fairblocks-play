    How to play

    Drag blocks onto the 8x8 slab. Fill a full row or column to clear it.

    • Gem is freed when its line clears.
    • Geode opens into a gem on the first clear; the next clear frees it.
    • Rock just takes up room until its line clears.

    Journey: free every gem before your moves run out. Zen: endless, chase your best. Daily: the same board for everyone. Clear several lines at once, or on back-to-back moves, for combo bonuses.
